## 中文

### 概述

数独 Sudoku（以下简称"本应用"）是一款**完全离线**的单机数独游戏。本应用不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息。

### 我们收集哪些信息

**我们不收集任何信息。**

本应用没有网络权限（Android 上未声明 `android.permission.INTERNET`），无法向任何服务器发送数据。

### 数据存储在哪里

以下数据仅保存在**您的设备本地**（Android 使用 SharedPreferences，iOS 使用 NSUserDefaults，桌面使用 java.util.prefs，Web 使用 localStorage）：

- 已解锁的关卡进度
- Pro 解锁状态
- 每日挑战完成日期与连续天数
- 游戏统计（对局数、通关数、最高分、各难度最佳成绩）
- 主题与语言偏好
- 游戏设置（自动擦除笔记、显示冲突）

卸载本应用即会删除上述全部数据。

### 内购

Pro 版本通过 **Google Play 结算**购买。付款、交易记录与退款由 Google 处理，适用 [Google 隐私权政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。本应用不会接触您的支付信息，仅在设备本地记录"已解锁"这一状态。

### 第三方服务

本应用**不集成**任何广告 SDK、分析 SDK、崩溃上报 SDK 或社交媒体 SDK。

唯一涉及第三方的功能是 Google Play 结算（仅 Android，且仅当您主动点击购买时）。

### 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

### 权限说明

| 权限 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| `com.android.vending.BILLING` | 仅用于处理 Pro 内购 |

本应用不请求网络、位置、存储、相机、麦克风、通讯录或通知权限。

### 政策变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新"最后更新"日期。由于本应用不收集数据，预计不会发生实质性变更。

## English

### Overview

Sudoku (the "App") is a **fully offline** single-player Sudoku game. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

### What we collect

**Nothing.**

The App has no network permission (it does not declare `android.permission.INTERNET` on Android), so it cannot send data to any server.

### Where data is stored

The following is stored **only on your device** (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults on iOS, java.util.prefs on desktop, localStorage on web):

- Unlocked level progress
- Pro unlock status
- Daily challenge completion date and streak
- Game statistics (games played, wins, best score, best score per difficulty)
- Theme and language preferences
- Gameplay settings (auto-erase notes, show conflicts)

Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data.

### In-app purchases

The Pro version is purchased through **Google Play Billing**. Payment, transaction records and refunds are handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App never sees your payment details; it only records an "unlocked" flag locally on your device.

### Third-party services

The App integrates **no** advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or social SDK.

The only third-party interaction is Google Play Billing (Android only, and only when you actively tap purchase).

### Children's privacy

The App collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

### Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `com.android.vending.BILLING` | Process the Pro in-app purchase |

The App does not request network, location, storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or notification permissions.

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date on this page will be updated. Since the App collects no data, material changes are not expected.
